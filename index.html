Why 21K and 22K gold?

Arabic and Middle Eastern jewelry is traditionally crafted in 21K (87.5% pure) and 22K (91.6% pure) gold — a richer color and higher intrinsic value than the 14K common in American mall jewelry. That means every piece holds its worth in gold, gram for gram. When you shop at Aryam’s Jewelry in Houston, the price you pay tracks the live gold market above, plus the craftsmanship of the piece itself.
