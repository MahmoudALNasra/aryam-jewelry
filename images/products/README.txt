Drop client product photos here (JPG/WebP).

Recommended:
- Portrait or square, at least 1200px on the long edge
- Under ~400 KB after compression
- Filename: sku-or-slug.jpg (e.g. ARY-BRD-001.jpg)

Then open /admin/ and set each product’s image URL to:
  ../images/products/your-file.jpg

Google Drive folder photos can be downloaded and placed here when available.
